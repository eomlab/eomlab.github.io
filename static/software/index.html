TOP

The Optothermal Photonic Retinal Prosthesis

TOP is a computational framework for studying gold-nanorod-mediated near-infrared photothermal neural stimulation. It links optical absorption and nanoscale heat generation to temperature-dependent membrane currents and neuronal excitation, providing a mechanistic bridge from photothermal physics to neural response.

Introduction

From photothermal stimulation to a retinal prosthesis

The retinal-prosthesis motivation is illustrated by our later work in ACS Nano (2025), where intravitreally delivered plasmonic nanorods were combined with patterned near-infrared illumination. The study demonstrated localized retinal activation and visually evoked cortical responses, supporting photothermal nanomaterials as a minimally invasive route toward spatially patterned retinal stimulation.

Nie J., Eom K.†, et al.
“Intravitreally Injected Plasmonic Nanorods Activate Bipolar Cells with Patterned Near-Infrared Laser Projection.”
ACS Nano, 2025, 19(12), 11823–11840.

Core software

Integrated biophysical model

The main software accompanies our Biophysical Journal (2018) study. The MATLAB implementation calculates NIR absorption and heat generation around gold nanorods, the resulting membrane-temperature dynamics, temperature-dependent TRPV1 current, heat-induced capacitive current, and the final membrane-potential response through a modified Hodgkin–Huxley model.
